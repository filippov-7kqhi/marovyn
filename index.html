About Marovyn

Held, Not Pushed

Founded by certified yoga therapist Sylvie Marchetti, Marovyn was built on a simple belief: a nervous system in survival mode doesn't need another workout. Every class is slow, fully prop-supported and designed to help your body actually downshift.

  • ✓ Certified yoga therapists with clinical training
  • ✓ Fully prop-supported, never a "modified" hard class
  • ✓ Trauma-informed, chronic-pain-aware teaching
  • ✓ Small classes with individual attention every session
